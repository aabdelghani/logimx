# Code of conduct

NotLogi follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and do not
harass, insult or demean anyone. Everyone is welcome, whatever their background or experience.

To report behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/aabdelghani). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may remove
comments, close issues or block people who do not follow it.
